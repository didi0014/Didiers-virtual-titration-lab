1. Set Up

Your analyte is hydrochloric acid (HCl). Your titrant is sodium hydroxide (NaOH). The HCl concentration is unknown to you.

A fresh unknown concentration will be assigned when you begin.

The simulation uses 25 °C, a 1:1 reaction, and 0.05 mL per drop. The equivalence point is approximately pH 7.00.
